# DoorMathPro Team Strategy & Business Review (Sept 2026)

## 1. Executive Summary
DoorMathPro began as a garage-door spring and engineering calculator. It has evolved into a broader field engineering and technical assistance platform designed specifically for garage-door technicians, installers, estimators, and service companies.

**THE CORE THESIS:** The calculator gets people in. Engineering creates value. AI creates differentiation. Training creates long-term value. Truck-stock matching creates workflow dependency. Documentation creates retention. Company accounts create recurring revenue.

## 2. What DoorMathPro Is
A mobile-first/PWA field engineering platform that brings together calculations, identification, technical reference information, AI assistance, inventory matching, documentation and training in one application.

- **Spring Scan:** AI-assisted wire-size identification
- **Engineer Spring:** IPPT, turns, wire size, ID, cut length, cycle-life
- **Door Weight:** Estimation using dimensions, construction, windows, struts
- **AI Door Scanner:** AI-assisted door/model identification from photographs
- **Truck Stock Matcher:** Matches engineering requirements against truck inventory
- **AI Technician Assistant:** Technical questions, troubleshooting, field support
- **Training:** Learning platform for apprentices and ongoing development
- **Spec Sheets & Job Log:** Professional documentation and audit trail
- **Reference Tools:** Wire charts, drum cross-reference, IPPT lookup

## 3. Product Design Priorities & Risks
- **Risk:** Too many features visible at once.
- **Mitigation:** Keep the primary workflow simple (Identify -> Calculate -> Engineer -> Match -> Document). Hide advanced tools under secondary navigation.
- **Positioning:** DOORMATHPRO — The Field Engineering & Technical Assistant for Garage Door Technicians.

## 4. Proposed Pricing Strategy (CAD)
- **FREE ($0):** Basic calculations, door weight, references. Acquisition tool.
- **PRO ($6.99/mo):** Individual technician. Unlimited AI scans, spec sheets, advanced tools.
- **CREW ($25/mo for 5 seats):** Small company. Shared truck inventory and job specs.
- **TEAM ($49/mo for 10 seats):** Larger company. Manager dashboard, reporting, admin controls.
- **LIFETIME ($165-$199):** Founding user limited offer to accelerate adoption.

## 5. Recommended Roadmap
- **Phase 1:** Garage Door Calculator (core engineering)
- **Phase 2:** Garage Door Technician Tool (Spring Scan, Door Weight, Truck Stock, Spec Sheets)
- **Phase 3:** Technical Intelligence Platform (AI Assistant + Manufacturer Database)
- **Phase 4:** Technician Training Platform (AI-powered lessons, scenarios, skill dev)
- **Phase 5:** Company Platform (Shared technicians, inventory, training, management)
- **Phase 6:** Industry Platform (Connecting technicians <-> companies <-> suppliers <-> manufacturers)

## 6. The Bigger Vision
The most valuable part of DoorMathPro may ultimately not be the calculator. It may be the creation of a digital knowledge layer for the garage-door industry.

**ULTIMATE VISION:** DoorMathPro becomes the technical intelligence layer of the garage-door industry — helping experienced technicians work faster, helping new technicians learn faster, and helping companies build a more knowledgeable and capable workforce.
